# AP Biology topic alignment

Framework: **AP Biology CED, effective Fall 2025**, listed for the 2026–27 school year. Checked November 8, 2026 against the [course page](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-biology), [CED](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-biology-course-and-exam-description.pdf), [course-at-a-glance topic codes](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-biology-course-at-a-glance.pdf), and [clarifications](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-biology-ced-clarifications-and-corrections.pdf).

All **60 numbered topics** map to one original introductory lesson, with two practice checks each. Lesson titles below are original paraphrases, not official topic titles or verbatim learning objectives. This mapping establishes topic coverage, not exhaustive coverage of all essential knowledge or official AP exam readiness. The course map also contains one original four-part written drill per unit.
