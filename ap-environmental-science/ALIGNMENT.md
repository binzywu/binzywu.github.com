## Using the practice

There are 72 original checks and 9 original written drills. These do not simulate a full exam, reproduce its complete task distribution or establish an official score. Use teacher instruction and official released work for assessment depth. Model diagrams and numbers are explicitly simplified; no empirical forecast or observed result is implied. Unit percentages refer only to the official multiple-choice content weighting.

Completed-check flags are isolated to this subject. Written drafts remain session-only. All lessons and models run offline; external primary-source links require internet. No visual browser QA has been performed.
