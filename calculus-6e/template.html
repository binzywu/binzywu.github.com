05 · A polynomial shadows a function

All polynomials here are centered at 0. Increase the degree, then move the evaluation point away from the center. Compare the measured error with a rigorous Lagrange bound.

Solid blue: original function · dashed orange: Taylor polynomial · vertical gray line: selected x. The plot shows x from −3 to 3.

For sin and cos, M = 1 is valid. For eˣ between 0 and x, M = exp(max(0, x)). The bound uses degree n even when a coefficient vanishes. At high degrees, very small differences may reach floating-point precision.

Trigonometry quick reference

Use radians in calculus: 180° = π radians. On the unit circle, (x,y) = (cos θ, sin θ), and sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.

For y = D + A cos(B(t−t₀)), the midline is D, amplitude is |A| and period is 2π/|B| when B ≠ 0. The shift t₀ places a peak when A > 0.

The book puts trigonometry review in Appendix A.3. This is a brief aid; the appendix’s full contents are not included here.
