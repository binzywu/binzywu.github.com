# Sixth-edition alignment

Target: **Calculus of a Single Variable, 6th edition**, Larson / Hostetler / Edwards. Standard edition, **ISBN 9780395885789**.

The map follows the [catalog contents](https://stpaulsepiscopal.ecampus.com/calculus-single-variable-6th-larson-ron/bk/9780395885789), with supporting numbering from a [contemporary sixth-edition syllabus](https://www.oc.nps.edu/ma1117/syllabus.html). The full book was not provided. This is an introductory topic map, not a claim to reproduce or inspect the complete textbook. All examples and problems in the app are original.

Preparation and Chapters 1–9 contain **72 numbered sections mapped to 73 lessons**. §P.4 has three model-family lessons. Series and some curve topics share lessons. Appendices, textbook exercise sets, projects and answer keys are not reproduced.
