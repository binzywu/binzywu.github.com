    Georgia Institute of Technology College Research

    Georgia Institute of Technology is a large public research university (STEM-dominant) in Atlanta, GA — warm climate, urban campus. It is one of the top engineering schools in the country, but with ~20,000+ undergrads it sits well above Runchen's stated <10k, campus-centered preference. He ranked it a 1 (top culture-fit) anyway — a notable profile-vs-prestige tension worth flagging against his "won't feel lost / worried about making friends at large anonymous schools" concern. On his list it is a Hi Reach. See runchen-wu-college-application-profile, runchen-college-list-draft-2026, and runchen-college-preferences-overall. Compare with peer research targets university-of-michigan-college-research (larger, also public/OOS) and case-western-reserve-university-college-research (small, strong BME).

    Links: - Admissions (first-year): https://admission.gatech.edu/first-year - Deadlines: https://admission.gatech.edu/first-year/deadlines - College of Engineering: https://coe.gatech.edu - Coulter Dept. of Biomedical Engineering (GT/Emory): https://bme.gatech.edu

    At a glance (2025-26 cycle; verify for his cycle)¶

    Item Detail
    Total undergrad ~20,000+ (4,050 in fall 2025 first-year class)
    Engineering college size College of Engineering is the largest unit; ~half+ of undergrads are in engineering/computing (exact count not verified here)
    Overall admit rate ~13% (8,819 admits / 66,912 apps, fall 2025)
    Non-resident (OOS) admit rate ~9% (vs ~30% for Georgia residents) — reported; verify
    Major-specific admit rate Not published as a separate rate, but major choice is an evaluated fit factor; CS/AE/ME are the most competitive intended majors
    SAT middle 50% 1370–1530 (total) — reported; per-section EBRW/Math split not verified
    Testing policy Test-REQUIRED — SAT or ACT must be submitted (self-report OK; ACT writing / SAT essay not needed)
    Admission type Holistic; NOT direct-admit-to-major, but declared major + demonstrated interest weighed for fit
    Application platform Common App only
    App plans & deadlines (2026-27) EA1 = GA residents only (Oct 15). Non-GA residents use EA2: apply by Nov 2, docs Nov 16, decision late Jan. RD Jan 6 (docs Jan 22, decision mid-March). Complete an EA deadline for merit-scholarship consideration.
    Essays Yes — personal essay(s) required via Common App
    Cost of attendance (2025-26) OOS on-campus total ~$56,300 (tuition ~$33,600); in-state ~$32,800. He is a WA resident → OOS. Need-based/merit aid for OOS is limited and competitive.

    Fit for Runchen — the strong side¶

    • Elite engineering + robotics/aerospace pedigree. GT is a national top program; his 7-year VEX spike (World Div. Champion + Builder Award, lead designer/builder/programmer) is exactly the maker/robotics profile GT values.
    • Real BME pathway. The Wallace H. Coulter Dept. of Biomedical Engineering (joint GT/Emory) is consistently top-ranked, and BME was a top-5 intended engineering major in the 2025 class — strong fit for his growing medical-devices/"needs electricity" interest without forcing him into wet-lab biology.
    • Stats broadly competitive. His SAT superscore 1550 sits at/above the 1370–1530 middle 50%; 3.982 GPA and 10 AP exams (six 5s) are strong. Test-required is fine for him since he has scores to submit.
    • Warm Atlanta climate — aligns with his dislike of cold.
    • Collaborative maker culture in robotics/engineering circles, project- and team-heavy (not a pure pre-professional grind on the CS-adjacent side).

    Fit for Runchen — the concerns (weigh seriously)¶

    • Size directly conflicts with his <10k, campus-centered, "won't feel lost" preference. At 20k+ undergrad this is his biggest structural mismatch; his #1 culture ranking is aspirational and should be pressure-tested against how he'd actually feel in large intro courses.
    • Low OOS odds. ~9% non-resident admit rate makes this a genuine reach even with his profile; GT strongly favors Georgia residents.
    • Math rigor clears the bar. GT's most selective engineering/CS applicants show BC or beyond — and he does reach beyond, via Advanced Calculus (MAH802) senior year, since Bellevue HS offers no AP Calculus BC (bellevue-school-district-math-pathway-and-advanced-calculus). GT weighs rigor in school context, so this is the top available track.
    • Cost. ~$56k/yr OOS with limited need/merit aid for non-residents — confirm affordability.
    • Intensity/scale. Big STEM environment can feel anonymous and competitive; not the small, high-touch setting he says he wants.

    Placement recommendation¶

    • Category: Reach for him as a non-resident (~9% OOS admit rate; strong profile but not a lock — the non-resident cap, not his coursework, is the binding constraint).
    • Rank: he placed it 1 (environment-fit view), but on program-prestige it is also elite (1). Flag the tension: the "1" fit rating sits uneasily with a 20k+ school given his explicit small-campus, fear-of-getting-lost preference. Worth an honest conversation before he over-weights it.
    • Practical notes: Common App only; must apply EA2 (Nov 2) as a non-Georgia resident to be in the strongest round and for merit consideration; test scores required; pick a major (BME or a robotics-adjacent eng major) and show demonstrated interest, since major fit is evaluated even though admission is not direct-to-major.
