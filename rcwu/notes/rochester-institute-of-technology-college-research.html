    Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT) is a private research university in Henrietta, NY (Rochester metro) — a large tech-focused school (~18,500 undergrads) built around career-integrated, co-op education. On Runchen's list it is a Likely / drop candidate (Rank 4): the engineering-and-hands-on-building identity fits him well on paper, but the size, cold, and co-op-heavy model cut against his stated preferences. See runchen-wu-college-application-profile, runchen-college-list-draft-2026, and runchen-college-preferences-overall. A warmer, smaller engineering peer he prefers is case-western-reserve-university-college-research.

    Fit for Runchen — the strong side

    • Engineering + hands-on/maker culture is core to RIT's identity — a strong match for a 7-year VEX builder/programmer with a robotics World Championship. RIT is not a cutthroat, weed-out prestige school; it is collaborative and applied.
    • Stats clear the bar comfortably. 3.982 GPA and 1550 SAT sit well above RIT medians; he is likely a merit-scholarship candidate.
    • Genuine BME fit. RIT offers a Biomedical Engineering BS (5-year, co-op) covering medical device design, instrumentation/imaging, physiological signal processing, and biomaterials — squarely the "engineering + medical devices, involves electricity" interest, without a wet-lab/biology-dominant orientation. Co-op employers include Medtronic, J&J, Moderna.
    • Admit odds are favorable, making this a low-anxiety Likely.
    • Common App accepted, so it adds little marginal application effort.

    Fit for Runchen — the concerns (weigh seriously)

    • Cold and snowy. Rochester winters are among the harshest of any school on his list — a direct hit on his strong cold-weather aversion.
    • Size. ~18,500 undergrads is far above his <~10k preference; the anonymous-large-campus friend-making worry applies (though RIT's tight major cohorts and clubs mitigate this somewhat).
    • Co-op is mandatory and time-shifting. BME requires ~48 weeks (four blocks) of co-op, typically extending to a 5-year timeline and pulling students off-campus for stretches — a structural feature he may not want.
    • Math rigor is fine here — and better than an AB-only reading implies: Bellevue HS offers no AP Calculus BC, and he takes post-BC Advanced Calculus (MAH802) senior year (bellevue-school-district-math-pathway-and-advanced-calculus).
    • Cost with unknown net price. Sticker ~$85k/yr; merit is available but unquantified, so real cost is uncertain until the NPC/aid offer.
    • Already flagged by him as a drop candidate, so all the above should be weighed against whether it earns a slot at all.
