    Runchen's Own Take

    Likes: close to Silicon Valley; more traditional college experience than hyper-competitive STEM schools (he notes this as an observation, not necessarily a positive). Dislikes: less cutting-edge feeling; fewer labs/research than big-name schools. Question: how competitive is the lifestyle, and how motivated are the students? (Motivated peers matter a lot to him — see runchen-college-preferences-overall.)

    Fit for Runchen — the strong side

    • Stats put him well above the bar. SAT 1550 is above SCU's ~75th percentile, and his 3.982 GPA far exceeds the ~3.72 enrolled average — a genuine safety/likely on stats.
    • Warm, campus-centered, and the right size. ~6,500 undergrad and a walkable campus directly satisfy his under-10k, dislikes-cold preferences.
    • Bioengineering major exists — a real hook for his medical-engineering pivot, plus a Healthcare Innovation and Design minor.

    Fit for Runchen — the concerns (weigh seriously)

    • He marked it a drop (Rank 4). His own read: "less cutting-edge," fewer labs/research than big names.
    • Bioengineering is solid but not a national BME powerhouse the way Case Western or Michigan are — no medical school on campus, smaller research scale.
    • Cost with likely thin aid. ~$89.7k/yr sticker; a high-stats full-pay family may see limited need aid.

    Placement recommendation

    • Category: Likely / low Target. On his stats this behaves like a likely school; the counselor's "warm SV safety" read is well-founded.
    • Rank: Program/prestige view lands around 3. His own environment-fit view is genuinely split: size, warmth, and collaborative culture argue for a 2, but he emotionally rated it a 4 (drop) on "not cutting-edge." Resolves toward keeping it as a comfortable floor unless he wants that slot for a better-fit target.
