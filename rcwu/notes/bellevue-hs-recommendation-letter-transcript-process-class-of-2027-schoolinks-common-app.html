    Bellevue HS Recommendation Letter & Transcript Process (Class of 2027 — SchooLinks

    Updated 2026-08-21
    college-applicationrecommendation-lettersrunchenbellevue-high-schoolschoollinkscommon-apptimeline

    How Runchen Wu (Bellevue High School, Bellevue WA, Class of 2027) requests teacher and counselor recommendation letters and transcripts, and how those flow to colleges. Researched live 2026-08-21 against bsd405.org and bellevuehigh.bsd405.org; the Common App recommender mechanics are the platform's standard behavior. Companion to the per-school rec requirements in Bioengineering major + robotics minor, essays & rec letters — by school (Fall 2027) and the deadlines in runchen-college-shortlist-and-tentative-application-plan-aug-2026.

    Platform: SchooLinks (Class of 2027), not Naviance

    • Class of 2027 and beyond use SchooLinks, the new state-wide college-and-career-readiness platform. Naviance is retired — it served only the Class of 2026, and beginning the 2026–27 school year all of grades 6–12 move to SchooLinks. (VERIFIED-LIVE: bsd405.org/programs-and-services/counseling/schoolinks and .../naviance.)
    • BHS/BSD does not use SCOIR, MaiaLearning, or Overgrad — no evidence of any of them on the district or school counseling pages.
    • Runchen, as Class of 2027, is on SchooLinks.

    Requesting recommendations — the teacher gets ONE request, not one per school

    This is the key point families get wrong: applying to many colleges does not mean the teacher fields many requests.

    1. Request each teacher once, inside the school platform (SchooLinks) — not by emailing them through Common App. You select the teacher and send a single request.
    2. The teacher writes one general letter and uploads it once. They complete a single "Teacher Evaluation" (ratings grid + letter). They do not get a separate task or email per college.
    3. The platform links to your Common App account (a one-time match + FERPA-waiver step). The school — counselor and teachers — then pushes the letter, transcript, and school report electronically to every college on your list. One letter → all schools, automatically.

    Common App recommender mechanics (the layer underneath)

    • Each college caps teacher recs (commonly 2; some allow 3; some want 0). You assign up to that cap per school — never more, but you may send fewer.
    • Assign the same teacher to multiple colleges freely. Runchen's plan of 1 STEM + 1 humanities teacher covers essentially every school on his list with those same two letters.
    • The letter is not customized per school in the teacher-evaluation flow — one general letter goes to all assigned colleges.
    • Assigning a teacher to a later-deadline school after they've already submitted works — Common App re-sends the existing letter; the teacher does nothing more.

    Transcripts

    • Requested through the platform (SchooLinks for Class of 2027), including for early-deadline schools. Not Parchment — no evidence BHS uses it.
    • The district public-records transcript page is for alumni/records requests, not the current-student application workflow.

    Deadlines and prep

    • By October 1 — complete any counselor forms for letters of recommendation and counselor reports; counselors then send their letters and school reports to all applicable schools. (Published on the BHS checklist, but written for the Naviance era — reconfirm for SchooLinks.)
    • Give each recommender a resume / brag sheet of activities and accomplishments.

    Caveats and who to ask

    • ⚠️ Every published procedural detail (the Oct 1 deadline, "request via platform," transcript flow) currently appears on the BHS site written for Naviance. The district confirmed the switch to SchooLinks but has not yet republished the step-by-step checklist. The mechanics are near-identical (SchooLinks integrates with Common App the way Naviance did), but the exact SchooLinks button-clicks and whether the Oct 1 date carries over are UNVERIFIED.
