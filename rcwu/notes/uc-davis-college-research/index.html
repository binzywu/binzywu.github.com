    UC Davis College Research

    University of California, Davis — a large public research university in Davis, CA (Central Valley, ~15 mi west of Sacramento), enrolling roughly 31,800–32,300 undergraduates. On Runchen's list it is his most realistic UC admit and the warm-weather UC; it sits as a Target. See the runchen-wu-college-application-profile, runchen-college-list-draft-2026, runchen-college-preferences-overall, and especially the UC-specific runchen-uc-gpa-and-uc-campus-analysis. Compare with his stronger-BME options like case-western-reserve-university-college-research and university-of-michigan-college-research.

    Links: UC Davis first-year admissions (ucdavis.edu/admissions/undergraduate/first-year); College of Engineering (engineering.ucdavis.edu); Biomedical Engineering (bme.ucdavis.edu); UC Application (apply.universityofcalifornia.edu).

    At a glance (2025-26 cycle; verify for his Fall-2027 cycle)¶

    Field Value
    Total undergrad ~31,800–32,300
    Engineering college undergrad Not separately confirmed; College of Engineering has 11 ABET majors and is described as one of the largest US engineering programs (likely ~5–6k undergrad — unverified)
    Overall admit rate ~44% (Fall 2025: 45,673 admits / 102,988 applicants)
    Engineering/major admit rate Not officially published per-major; engineering is less impacted at Davis than at Berkeley/UCLA (see below)
    SAT middle 50% N/A — test-blind, scores not read at all (Regents dropped SAT/ACT in 2020)
    Testing policy Test-free / test-blind (definitive)
    Admission type Holistic; you select a major on the UC Application, and engineering/CS selectivity runs above the campus rate. No interviews
    Application platform UC Application (its own portal — NOT Common App)
    App plans & deadlines No ED/EA/RD. Single filing window Oct 1–Nov 30 (2026-27 dates); app opens Aug 1. One decision round
    Essays required 4 Personal Insight Questions (PIQs), choose 4 of 8, 350 words each (shared across all UC campuses)
    Cost of attendance Nonresident (WA = OOS) est. ~$73–76k/yr — verify exact 2026-27 figure; no financial aid or merit for nonresidents (definitive)

    Fit for Runchen — the strong side¶

    • Best UC admit odds by far. At ~44% overall (vs. ~8–9% at Berkeley/UCLA, ~28% UCSD), Davis is the one UC that reads as a genuine Target rather than a lottery — the point of keeping it on the list.
    • Real biomedical-engineering fit. UC Davis has a standalone ABET BME department (~500 undergrads) with medical-device development among its focus areas, plus imaging centers and the Tech Foundry maker space. This aligns with his medical-engineering pivot better than a generic large public.
    • Warm-ish and campus-centered feel. Central Valley climate (hot summers, mild winters) satisfies his cold-weather aversion — a clear contrast to the Case/Michigan/WPI cold cluster. Davis is a classic self-contained "college town," bike-centric, generally collaborative and non-cutthroat reputation.
    • Cheap to add. Per his UC strategy, an extra campus costs only the ~$80 fee on an application he's already filing for other UCs.

    Fit for Runchen — the concerns (weigh seriously)¶

    • Size contradicts his <10k, "won't-feel-lost" preference. ~32k undergrad is large and anonymous by his stated criteria — the same objection as UCSD, UW, and Michigan. Mitigant: engineering cohort, BME (~500), and project teams are smaller communities.
    • Test-blind erases his strongest asset. His 1550 SAT counts for nothing; all weight shifts to UC GPA, rigor, PIQs, and the VEX Worlds/robotics spike. See runchen-uc-gpa-and-uc-campus-analysis.
    • Nonresident headwind + no aid. UC caps OOS enrollment and holds nonresidents to a higher bar (min 3.4 GPA vs 3.0 in-state), and gives zero aid to nonresidents — full sticker ~$73–76k/yr, like Michigan.
    • BME is solid but not top-tier. Good fit, but Davis BME does not carry the prestige of Case's Human Fusions or Michigan's Michigan-Medicine-joint BME — if BME is the priority, those rank higher.
    • Math is a non-issue here — and stronger than the AB-only reading suggests: Bellevue HS offers no AP Calculus BC, and he takes post-BC Advanced Calculus (MAH802) senior year (bellevue-school-district-math-pathway-and-advanced-calculus).

    Placement recommendation¶

    • Category: Target (for him specifically as a non-resident — the only UC that isn't a Reach). ~44% campus rate, engineering somewhat above that but still attainable with his profile.
    • Rank (1–4 scale): Program-prestige view ~3 (respectable, not elite engineering/BME). His own environment-fit view is mixed: warm + collaborative town pulls it up, but ~32k size and anonymity pull it down — net ~3, consistent with how the UC analysis note ranks it. Note the usual profile-vs-prestige tension is milder here since it's neither a top-prestige nor a great-fit standout — it's the pragmatic "safe-ish UC" slot.
    • Practical notes: Uses the UC Application, not Common App; must declare a major (select engineering/BME); reuses the same 4 PIQs as all UCs; single Nov 30 deadline, no early option.
