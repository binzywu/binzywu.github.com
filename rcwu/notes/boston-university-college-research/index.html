    Boston University College Research

    Boston University — large private research university in Boston, MA. ~18,250 undergrads (a medium-large school, well above Runchen's "under-10k, campus-centered" preference) on an urban campus straddling Commonwealth Ave. It sits on his list as a Target — but Runchen himself marked it a drop candidate (Rank 4) for environment reasons (big, urban, cold). See runchen-wu-college-application-profile, runchen-college-list-draft-2026, and runchen-college-preferences-overall. Compare with the other Boston-area engineering options he's weighing, tufts-university-college-research and worcester-polytechnic-institute-college-research.

    Links: Admissions (bu.edu/admissions); College of Engineering (bu.edu/eng); Biomedical Engineering (bu.edu/eng — BME dept); Deadlines (bu.edu/admissions/apply/deadlines).

    Fit for Runchen — the strong side¶

    • Stats are comfortably in range — arguably above the class. His SAT 1550 (760/790) sits at or above the 75th percentile on both sections (750/780); 3.982 GPA and 10 AP exams are competitive. On numbers alone he is a strong applicant, which is why the counselor tiers it Target rather than Reach.
    • Genuine biomedical-engineering fit. BU has one of the oldest BME departments in the world (40+ faculty, ranked ~#18 nationally) — a real match to his growing medical-devices / "needs to involve electricity" interest, without the wet-lab/biology emphasis he dislikes.
    • Boston medical-biotech ecosystem (hospitals, Longwood, biotech firms) mirrors the same proximity advantage he liked about Tufts — strong for medical-engineering internships and research.
    • Collaborative, non-cutthroat, non-sports-dominated culture — BU has no Division-I football and a large, cooperative student body; that broadly fits his dislike of cutthroat/party/sports-jock environments.
    • Direct-to-college admission means he can apply straight into Engineering/BME and signal his robotics-to-medical-devices spike coherently.

    Fit for Runchen — the concerns (weigh seriously)¶

    • Size vs. his <10k preference. ~18,250 undergrad is roughly double his stated ceiling — precisely the "large, anonymous, hard-to-make-friends" worry he flagged. This is his single biggest objection and the reason he self-marked it a drop.
    • Cold weather + urban, non-contained campus. Boston winters cut against his explicit dislike of cold; BU's campus is a linear urban strip along a major avenue rather than the enclosed, campus-centered feel he prefers.
    • Cost with little relief for his family. ~$98k/yr sticker; BU merit is limited (~10% of aid) and, as a likely full-pay family, need aid is unlikely — expensive relative to fit.
    • Robotics is not a headline strength. No standalone undergraduate robotics major on par with Michigan; his VEX spike reads as general engineering strength rather than program-matched here.
    • Math rigor is a non-concern. Bellevue HS offers no AP Calculus BC, and he takes post-BC Advanced Calculus (MAH802) senior year (bellevue-school-district-math-pathway-and-advanced-calculus) — comfortably above BU's expectations.

    Placement recommendation¶

    • Category: Target for him — his stats sit at/above the middle 50%, though ~11% overall admit rate keeps any school in this range non-trivial.
    • Rank (his 1–4 scale): Rank 4 (drop candidate) per Runchen's own environment-fit view — size, cold, and urban campus all contradict stated preferences. Note the tension: academically/BME-wise it's a solid Target with strong odds, but on personal-fit criteria he ranks it near the bottom. If application slots are tight, better-fit BME options (Case Western, Michigan BME) or smaller Boston-area schools (Tufts, WPI) likely serve him better.
    • Practical note: Must declare a school/college — apply directly to Engineering (BME). To be considered for merit + Kilachand Honors, materials are due Dec 1, earlier than RD.
