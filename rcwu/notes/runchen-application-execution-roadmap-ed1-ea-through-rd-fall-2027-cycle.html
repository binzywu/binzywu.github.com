    Execution roadmap for Runchen's Fall-2027 entry applications, replacing the exploration phase. Built from the round assignments he set on 2026-09-19 and the researched essay master list and shortlist. The single ED goes to UPenn (ED1); Harvey Mudd is the ED2 fallback; Cornell drops to RD — this resolves the open Cornell-vs-Penn ED question from the shortlist note.

    The two things to internalize first

    1. UW (Nov 15) and the UCs (Nov 30) are NOT a later phase. Their deadlines land two-to-four weeks after Nov 1, inside the same crunch. Treat November as one continuous sprint, not three.
    2. Everything hinges on the UPenn ED1 decision (~mid-December). If admitted, it's binding — withdraw everything else. If deferred/denied, the Cornell RD, Harvey Mudd ED2, and UC applications must already be drafted and ready to fire in early January. Draft the January apps during the November crunch, not after.

    Phase 1 — The November crunch (now → Nov 30)

    Task Zero, this week (by ~Sept 26)

    • Open the Common App and pull every live supplement prompt. All the "⚠️ verify after Aug 1" rows in the master list are now due — the real 2026-27 prompts exist today. Confirm UPenn's SEAS essay, USC's Quick Takes, Santa Clara, and Georgia Tech wording verbatim.

    Essay battle plan (priority order)

    The Common App 650 is the master asset — it feeds UPenn, GT, Case Western, USC, WPI, Santa Clara, Northeastern, RIT, and Cornell. Sequence:

    1. Common App 650 — finalize first. Everything downstream depends on it. Apply the essay strategy: it's the "who you are" essay; don't burn the robotics material here if a supplement wants it.
    2. UPenn supplements (ED1) — highest stakes, due Nov 1. Thank-you note + community-at-Penn + SEAS. This is the bet; it gets the most polish.
    3. UC PIQs (4 × 350) — best effort-to-coverage ratio on the list (1,400 words → all UC campuses, due Nov 30). PIQ #3 (talent/skill) or #6 (subject that inspires) carry the robotics→medical-devices throughline.
    4. Georgia Tech 300 (why-major/why-GT) and USC supplemental set — short, due Nov 1-2.
    5. UW writing — once the platform is resolved (Nov 15).
    6. Cornell's 6 pieces (800 words) — the heaviest single load; draft in November even though it's a Jan 2 RD, so it's ready if ED1 fails. Prompts 5 (meaningful activity) and 6 (meaningful award) are near-gifts for a VEX Worlds competitor.
    7. Harvey Mudd 500 + 100 — draft in parallel, submit only as ED2 if needed.

    "Optional" is not optional (College Essay Strategy — Common App and Supplements (Cosmic 2026)): complete every optional supplement. No last-minute rewrites — anything finished inside the final week of a deadline gets a third-party read, not a panic edit.

    Demonstrated interest

    Santa Clara and RIT track interest; so does Georgia Tech's evaluation ("genuine interest in GT"). Log any info sessions / visits / portal engagement as application inputs, not just research.

    Phase 2 — The December ED gate (~Dec 15)

    UPenn releases the ED1 decision in mid-December. This is the branch point.

    • Admitted → binding. Withdraw all EA/RD applications, do not submit Cornell RD or Harvey Mudd ED2, deposit at Penn. Done.
    • Deferred → Penn re-reviews in RD. Proceed with the full RD/ED2 plan below; optionally send a brief deferral update (new grades/awards) if Penn allows.
    • Denied → proceed with the RD/ED2 plan below.

    By mid-December the EA results also start arriving (Case Western, WPI, Northeastern, Santa Clara, RIT, GT roll in Dec-Feb) — these are non-binding and can only help morale/options, not lock anything.

    Phase 3 — January (only if UPenn ED1 does not admit)

    • Cornell RD (Jan 2) — submit the 6-piece supplement drafted in November. His original visit favorite; strong engineering fit.
    • Harvey Mudd ED2 (Jan 5) — binding second shot. Only submit if he'd genuinely attend over all pending RD schools and finances work (ED = no aid comparison). Mudd weights essays and fit heavily.
    • Confirm nothing else on the list has a January RD deadline that got missed (the current 13-school list does not — UW and UCs already closed in November).

    Phase 4 — Decisions & the deposit (Feb → May 1)

    • Results timeline: EA (Dec-Feb) → Harvey Mudd ED2 (~mid-Feb) → UW (Mar 1-15) → UCs (late March) → Cornell RD (late March).
    • Compare admits on fit + net cost. UW is the in-state financial anchor; the UCs are OOS full-pay; private admits vary on merit/need aid.
    • Deposit at one school by May 1, 2027; decline the rest.
