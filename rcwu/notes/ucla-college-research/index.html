    UCLA College Research

    The University of California, Los Angeles is a very large public flagship (~33k undergrad) in the Westwood neighborhood of Los Angeles — the opposite of the small, campus-centered school Runchen prefers, but a marquee Hi Reach on his list. As a Washington resident he is a non-resident here: UC is test-blind (his 1550 SAT counts for nothing) and gives no financial aid to nonresidents. See the UC GPA & campus analysis (his UC capped-weighted ≈4.29) and the profile. Warm-weather sibling to the colder Michigan and Case Western on his list.

    Links: Admissions; Samueli School of Engineering; Bioengineering; Cost of attendance

    At a glance (2025-26 cycle; verify for his Fall-2027 cycle)¶

    Field Value
    Total undergrad ~33,000 (Fall 2023: 33,040)
    Engineering (Samueli) undergrad ~3,000–3,500 across 7 majors (unverified exact figure); Bioengineering dept ~288 undergrads
    Overall admit rate ~9% (Fall 2025; 145,060 freshman applicants)
    Engineering/major admit rate Not officially published; CS & Engineering are impacted → materially harder than 9% headline, esp. OOS
    SAT middle 50% N/A — test-blind. Scores not read for admission or scholarships
    Testing policy Test-blind / test-free (definitive)
    Admission type Holistic, admitted to a specific major/campus — must declare (some specialty-school majors need a supplemental app)
    Application platform UC Application (not Common App)
    App plans & deadlines No ED/EA. Single filing window: application Aug 1, submit Nov 1–30 for Fall entry (verify exact Fall-2027 dates)
    Essays 4 Personal Insight Questions (of 8), 350 words each
    Cost (non-resident, 2025-26) ~$82.4k on-campus / ~$84.7k off-campus (incl. ~$37.6k NRST); CA-resident ~$44.8k. No aid for nonresidents — full sticker

    Fit for Runchen — the strong side¶

    • Robotics/engineering strength travels well. His 7-year VEX spike (Worlds Division Champion + Builder Award) and rigorous transcript are exactly the kind of achievement holistic UC review rewards, and with the SAT off the table his robotics + medical-engineering narrative in the PIQs is his primary lever.
    • Bioengineering is a genuine medical-engineering fit. UCLA BE sits "at the interface of engineering, medicine, and basic sciences" with research in smart medical devices, stents, and bioelectronics — aligned with his "medical applications, needs to involve electricity" interest (not wet-lab biology). It's a small department (~288 undergrads) inside the big university.
    • Warm climate is a clear plus — LA weather directly satisfies his dislike of cold, unlike Michigan/Case/WPI/RPI.
    • Low marginal cost to apply — per his UC strategy, adding UCLA is only the ~$80 campus fee on the shared UC application.

    Fit for Runchen — the concerns (weigh seriously)¶

    • Size badly contradicts his <10k, "won't-feel-lost" preference — ~33k undergrad, same objection as UCSD/UW/Michigan. Mitigants (small BE cohort, project teams) exist but the anonymity worry is real.
    • Impacted major + OOS = double headwind. Engineering/CS admit bars sit well below the 9% campus rate, and UC holds nonresidents to a higher bar. His UC capped-weighted ~4.29 is in-range-but-below-median for UCLA engineering; realistically a lottery reach.
    • Test-blind erases his best asset. The 1550 superscore that helps elsewhere is invisible here.
    • Cost with no aid: ~$82–85k/yr full sticker for a WA family — same no-nonresident-aid problem as Michigan.
    • Math track is maximal for his school — Bellevue HS offers no AP Calculus BC, and he takes post-BC Advanced Calculus (MAH802) senior year (bellevue-school-district-math-pathway-and-advanced-calculus). UC comprehensive review scores rigor against available offerings, so this is not a weakness.

    Placement recommendation¶

    • Category: Reach (lottery reach for a non-resident into an impacted major; effective odds below the 9% headline).
    • Rank (his 1–4 scale): Program prestige a 1 (elite, warm, credible BE fit). On his own environment-fit criteria (size, anonymity) more like a 3 — the same profile-vs-prestige tension flagged for UCSD, Georgia Tech, and Michigan. He ranked UCLA a 3 in the UC analysis.
    • Practical: UC Application, not Common App; must declare a major; write the 4 PIQs to carry the robotics/medical-engineering story since scores can't help.
