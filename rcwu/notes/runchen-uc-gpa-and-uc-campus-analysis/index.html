    Runchen UC GPA and UC Campus Analysis

    How Runchen looks to the University of California specifically, which recalculates GPA its own way and is test-blind. Analyzed against the four UC campuses on his draft list (Berkeley, UCLA, San Diego, Davis). Source data: the final runchen-hs-transcript-july-2026-final-g11.

    UC GPA method (how it differs from the 3.982 cumulative)¶

    • Only 10th & 11th grade UC-approved a-g courses count. 9th grade, senior year, and middle-school courses are excluded — so his freshman AP Modern World History and his 8th-grade accelerated courses don't factor in.
    • Grades: A=4, B=3, C=2 per semester.
    • Honors bonus (+1/semester) for AP / UC-approved honors courses, capped at 8 semesters total, with no more than 4 from 10th grade.
    • Not a-g (excluded): PE (Foundations of Fitness, Net & Wall Sports) and Health. Assumption: Percussion Ensemble counted as a-g "f" (visual/performing arts) — see sensitivity note.

    The calculation (24 a-g semesters, grades 10–11)¶

    Grade a-g semesters Grade points AP/honors semesters
    10 (all A) 14 56 8 (→ capped to 4)
    11 (9 A, 1 B in AP Lit S2) 10 39 6
    Total 24 95 14 raw → 8 capped

    Runchen's three UC GPAs:

    UC GPA type Value What it's used for
    Unweighted 3.96 Raw a-g, no bonus
    Capped weighted 4.29 The primary number UC reports/compares
    Fully weighted (uncapped) 4.54 Context at some campuses

    Sensitivity: if Percussion Ensemble is not a-g, the figures shift slightly to ~3.95 unweighted / ~4.35 capped weighted (fewer non-honors A's dilute the honors bonus). Either way, capped weighted ≈ 4.3. The single B in AP Lit (11th S2) is the only non-A in the entire UC window and is fully priced in.

    Insights¶

    1. His 1550 SAT counts for nothing at the UCs. They are test-blind — scores are not read at all. This is the single biggest UC-specific fact: it removes one of his strongest assets and shifts all weight onto GPA, course rigor, the four Personal Insight Questions (PIQs), and activities. His VEX Worlds spike and medical-engineering narrative have to carry the application.

    2. His UC capped-weighted ~4.29 is solid but not a standout for the top UCs. Admitted engineering/CS students at Berkeley and UCLA routinely sit at ~4.3–4.5 capped, and those majors are impacted (a higher bar than the campus-wide rate). ~4.29 is competitive for UCSD and Davis, in-range-but-below-median for a Berkeley/UCLA engineering reach — especially as a non-resident.

    3. Out-of-state is a real headwind, twice over. UC caps nonresident enrollment and holds OOS applicants to a higher bar; Berkeley/UCLA engineering OOS admit rates are effectively single digits. And UC gives no financial aid to nonresidents — full sticker (~$70k+/yr) like Michigan.

    4. Major impaction matters more than the headline rate. Applying into engineering/CS at Berkeley/UCLA/UCSD is materially harder than the campus's overall admit rate implies. Declared-major bar > campus bar.

    5. Size contradicts his stated fit. All four UCs are large (UCSD ~35k, the others 30k+) — the opposite of his "under-10k, won't-feel-lost" preference (see runchen-college-preferences-overall). Notably he ranked UCSD a 1 despite this — the same profile-vs-prestige tension flagged for Georgia Tech and Michigan.

    6. The B is minor here too. It's literally the one non-A in 24 semesters; it drops the unweighted from 4.0 to 3.96 and is invisible in the capped-weighted headline. Not a factor in UC outcomes.

    7. Strategy — cheap breadth, managed expectations. Per his own UC strategy, extra campuses cost only the ~$80/campus fee. Rational play: treat Davis (and UCSD) as the realistic UC targets, Berkeley/UCLA as lottery reaches (accept the OOS+impaction odds), and lean the PIQs hard on the robotics/medical-engineering story since the SAT can't help.
