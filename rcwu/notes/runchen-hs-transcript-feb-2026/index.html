    Runchen HS Transcript Feb 2026

    Superseded by runchen-hs-transcript-july-2026-final-g11 (printed 2026-07-08), which has the complete, graded junior year. Keep this note only as the mid-year snapshot.

    Official Bellevue High School (Bellevue, WA) unofficial transcript for Runchen Wu, printed 2026-02-03, Grade 11. Perm ID 3012169; DOB 2009-04-21.

    Cumulative HS GPA on this snapshot: 4.000 — but note this print date is mid-11th-grade: the Semester 2 courses were still "Work In Progress" and ungraded. This snapshot therefore predates the B in AP Literature & Composition (11th-grade S2 finals) that later broke the 4.0. See the profile for the current GPA status.

    Runchen began earning high-school credit in 8th grade at Odle Middle School (accelerated "AL" courses), giving him a long runway of advanced coursework.

    Notes¶

    • The AP course sequence on the transcript reconciles with the exam scores in runchen-ap-exam-scores-2026: AP Modern World Hist → World History 5, AP US Hist → US History 5, AP Comp Sci → 5, AP Physics 1 → 5, AP Lang → 4, AP Calc AB → 5, AP Chemistry → 4, AP Lit → 4, AP US Gov → 4.
    • AP Chinese (5, freshman year) does not appear as a course here — consistent with it being taken as an exam by a heritage speaker rather than via a school course; French 2–4 fulfills the world-language sequence on the transcript.
    • Math sequence reaches AP Calculus AB in 11th grade, followed by Advanced Calculus (MAH802) in 12th. Bellevue HS does not offer AP Calculus BC, and Advanced Calculus covers post-BC/multivariable material — so this is the most advanced math his school offers, not a gap.
