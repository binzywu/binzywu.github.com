    Runchen — Robotics Competition Journey

    Runchen's VEX robotics career spans 2019–present as a robot designer, builder, and programmer. Part of the runchen-wu-college-application-profile.

    Volunteering & Advising¶

    • Volunteers in general robotics education — teaches elementary and middle school students robotics knowledge and skills through both formal classes and informal coaching.
    • Builder Adviser in his current org, advising all competition teams.

    Competition History¶

    High School (Steamoji Robotics)¶

    • 2023–24 (Over Under) — Team 10W: Washington State Champion; qualified for the World Championship.
    • 2024–25 (High Stakes): State semi-finalist; World Championship Division Champion.
    • 2025–26 (Push Back) — Team 1523W: State semi-finalist; qualified for the World Championship via the Think Award at the State Championship.

    Middle School (Blaze Robotics Academy)¶

    • Team 938W. Joined Blaze Robotics Academy after his family moved; he was the only member with prior competition experience. The core teammates stayed together for ~3 years; the main driver, Jash, left for a year but returned and still works with Runchen today.
    • 2022–2023: Became the best middle school team in Washington.
    • 2021–2022 (Tipping Point): Competed at the World Championship in Dallas, TX — his 3rd time qualifying. Ranked 6th but lost in the divisional round of 16. Also his first out-of-state competition (US Open).
    • 2020–2021 (Change Up): First season in V5RC; enjoyed working as a team.

    Elementary School¶

    • Team 52586A (2019–2020). After a summer camp, started a private team with two same-age teammates (parent-funded). Competed in 6 tournaments including the state championship. Learned to build robots, collaborate, organize/participate in competitions, and program.
    • Washington State Championship Tournament Champion; 4 total awards. Qualified for the World Championship, but the event was cancelled due to COVID.

    Tools¶

    Fusion 360 (robotics + 3D printing), plus 3D design, modeling, and printing. See runchen-music-activities and runchen-soccer-and-school-clubs for other activities.
