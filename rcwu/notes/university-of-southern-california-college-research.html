    University of Southern California (USC) — a large private research university in Los Angeles, CA (~21,000 undergrads), with engineering housed in the Viterbi School of Engineering. On Runchen's list this is a Reach that he himself marked a DROP candidate (Rank 4) — he wants to steer clear of the Greek/party/celebrity/big-time-football scene USC is known for. See the runchen-wu-college-application-profile, the runchen-college-list-draft-2026, and runchen-college-preferences-overall. Warm-weather private peer to his other large-school reaches like university-of-michigan-college-research.

    Links: USC Admission (admission.usc.edu); Viterbi School of Engineering (viterbischool.usc.edu); Viterbi Undergraduate Admission (viterbiadmission.usc.edu); Biomedical Engineering (viterbiadmission.usc.edu/programs/undergraduate/majors/biomedical-engineering).

    Fit for Runchen — the strong side

    • Stats are in range. SAT 1550 sits right at the top of the middle-50% band (EBRW 760 = 75th %ile, Math 790 = 75th %ile); GPA 3.982 and the VEX Worlds spike make him a credible — if still reach-level — applicant.
    • Warm LA climate directly satisfies his dislike of cold weather (a clean win vs. the Case/Michigan/WPI cold-weather cluster).
    • Real medical-engineering ecosystem. Viterbi BME sits near the Keck School of Medicine and USC's medical-device/health-tech research; the BME senior capstone (BME 405) has teams building a biomedical device end-to-end, plus biomedical imaging (BME 426) and tissue-engineering coursework — a legitimate outlet for his "medical applications of engineering, needs electricity" interest.
    • Strong overall engineering + robotics presence and a well-resourced private research university with deep industry ties.

    Fit for Runchen — the concerns (weigh seriously)

    • He flagged it a DROP (Rank 4). The core reason is cultural: USC's prominent Greek life (~large share of students), celebrity/entertainment vibe, and big-time football (now Big Ten) are exactly the party/sports-dominated scene he wants to avoid.
    • Size well over his <10k preference. ~21,000 undergrads cuts against his "small, campus-centered, won't-get-lost" priority and his worry about making friends at large anonymous schools. (Viterbi's smaller ~3k engineering cohort is a partial mitigant, but the overall environment is large.)
    • Cost with no residency break. ~$99.6k/yr sticker; as a private, WA residency gives no discount, and merit (Trustee/Presidential) is long-odds. Need-based aid depends on family finances.
    • Reach-level selectivity (~10% overall) — admission is genuinely uncertain even for a strong applicant.
    • BME fit is good but not best-in-class for him relative to Case Western or Michigan's medicine-integrated BME; USC BME is solid, not a standout differentiator.
