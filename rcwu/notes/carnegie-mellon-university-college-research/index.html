    Carnegie Mellon University — College Research

    Carnegie Mellon University — Pittsburgh, PA (urban). A reach school and arguably the strongest robotics-engineering fit in the US for Runchen's profile. Part of the runchen-college-counseling-working-school-list-student-profile; see also runchen-wu-college-application-profile, runchen-robotics-competition-journey, and runchen-college-preferences-overall.

    Key Stats¶

    • Undergrad enrollment: ~7,824 (Fall 2024, all campuses)
    • Acceptance rate: ~11.7% overall (3,959 of 33,941, Fall 2024). Robotics sits in the School of Computer Science (SCS), whose admit rate is only ~3–5%; College of Engineering also runs harder than the overall rate.
    • Middle 50% GPA: ~3.95–4.0 unweighted (class avg 3.89; 94% in top 10%)
    • Middle 50% SAT: 1510–1560 (median 1540); SAT Math 770–800
    • Middle 50% ACT: 34–35 (median 35)
    • Student–faculty ratio: 6:1
    • Cost of attendance: ~$87,670 (2025–26); typically $90K+ all-in
    • Deadlines: ED I Nov 1 (only ED round, no ED II); RD Jan 1 (Dec 1 Drama/Music); no Early Action. Test-required.

    Fit & Admissions¶

    Runchen's 3.95 GPA / 1550 SAT superscore sits inside the middle 50% — competitive but not a lock (96% of enrolled scored 1400+, ~85% had 3.75+ GPA). Engineering/robotics bar runs higher than 11.7%. His 7-year VEX run with state titles and World Championship qualifications is exactly the differentiator CMU's holistic review rewards. ED I improves odds (~13.8% ED vs ~10% RD) and is worth strong consideration if CMU is the clear #1.

    Why It Fits¶

    • Robotics Institute — largest and oldest in the world (founded 1979), 100+ faculty (Matt Mason, Howie Choset, Manuela Veloso, Martial Hebert).
    • Robotics Innovation Center (opened 2024) — industry-grade labs accessible to undergrads.
    • Undergrad research pathways: SURF, RISS, plus a BS in Robotics as an additional major.
    • 6:1 ratio, small-section culture → real faculty access.
    • 5th-year integrated master's (IMB) in ECE/MechE.
    • Top recruiting: Google, Meta, NVIDIA, Boston Dynamics, Tesla; strong Pittsburgh AV/robotics startup scene. ~95–97% post-grad outcomes; median eng starting salary ~$95K–110K (CS-adjacent often $120K+).

    Concerns¶

    • Brutally selective (especially SCS/engineering); 1550/3.95 is in-range, not above.
    • Famously intense workload + documented stress/mental-health reputation — a shift from his balanced HS profile (soccer, SYSO, rock band, piano).
    • ~$87–90K/year; aid less generous than some peers (Princeton/MIT/Harvard) for upper-middle-income WA families.
    • Pittsburgh weather/vibe a real change from Bellevue PNW.
    • ED I is binding, Nov 1, no ED II or EA.

    Runchen's Own Take¶

    Likes: big tech in the area; lots of engineering to do; interdisciplinary program; clean, good-looking campus. Dislikes: might be too busy; classes will be hard. Worry: Does the campus feel busy or stressful day-to-day? He's wary of a "Suits / law-firm" vibe with no small talk — echoing the AI commentary about CMU being a busy, intense place. This intensity concern is his single biggest hesitation about CMU.

    Application Requirements¶

    • Common App or Coalition App.
    • 1 counselor + 2 teacher recs (a STEM teacher strongly recommended).
    • Supplemental essays (300 words each): Why CMU/this program; intellectual growth outside the classroom (tie to VEX/mentoring); a community you contribute to (robotics, SYSO, or soccer).
    • Test scores required (CMU is one of few top schools requiring them). No interview. Mid-Year Report due February.

    Current-Cycle Research (2026-07-24)¶

    Carnegie Mellon is arguably the single strongest robotics-engineering fit in the country for Runchen, and he ranks it 3 ("as intense as MIT") — his own biggest hesitation is that it may simply be too hard. Sits in the Hi Reach tier of the runchen-college-list-draft-2026.

    At a glance (2025-26 cycle; verify for his Fall-2027 cycle)¶

    Field Value
    Total undergrad ~7,728 (Fall 2025, Pittsburgh campus; CDS 2025-26)
    College of Engineering size ~large sub-college (exact undergrad count not pulled — verify)
    Overall admit rate ~11.1% (3,859 admitted / 34,867 applied, Fall 2025)
    Major-specific admit rate Robotics lives in School of Computer Science (SCS) — commonly cited ~3-5%, not officially published; College of Engineering also runs tougher than 11.1% but CMU does not release per-college rates
    SAT middle 50% Composite 1500-1560 (median 1540); EBRW 730-770; Math 770-800 (CDS 2025-26)
    Testing policy Test-required-ish, by college: SCS requires SAT/ACT; College of Engineering is "test-flexible" (must submit a test — SAT/ACT/IB/AP/A-Level — but you choose the type). Not test-optional for eng.
    Admission type Direct-to-college — you apply to a specific college (e.g. CoE or SCS); holistic review
    Application platform Common App exclusively
    App plans & deadlines ED Nov 2 (notify Dec 15, reply Feb 1); RD Jan 4 (notify by Apr 1). No ED II, no Early Action.
    Cost of attendance ~$93,614 all-in for 2026-27 (first-year, on-campus; tuition ~$69,702). Private, so WA residency is irrelevant. Need-based aid meets need but CMU is less generous than MIT/Ivies for upper-middle-income families; essentially no merit aid.

    Fit for Runchen — the strong side¶

    • Best-in-world robotics pedigree. Robotics Institute (largest/oldest, founded 1979), Robotics Innovation Center (2024), a BS in Robotics as an additional major, plus SURF/RISS research pipelines — a genuine spike match for his 7-year VEX run + VEX Worlds Division Champion.
    • His stats are in range. SAT superscore 1550 sits at/above the 1540 median; GPA 3.982 beats the 3.89 enrolled average (43.9% of enrolled had a 4.0, so his single B is unremarkable here).
    • Mid-size + campus-centered. At ~7.7k undergrad it actually satisfies his <10k, won't-get-lost preference — a real advantage over Michigan, UCSD, and UW on his list.

    Fit for Runchen — the concerns (weigh seriously)¶

    • Intensity is the headline risk — and it's his own #1 worry. He explicitly fears it's "as intense as MIT" and "too hard." This is the dominant reason it's a drop-candidate for him despite the elite program.
    • BME fit is weaker than it looks. CMU offers Biomedical Engineering only as an additional major/minor, not a standalone primary degree — for a primary BME home, case-western-reserve-university-college-research and Michigan are cleaner fits.
    • Math rigor is not the soft spot it first appeared. Bellevue HS offers no AP Calculus BC; his senior course is Advanced Calculus (MAH802), post-BC and multivariable, with Bellevue College credit (bellevue-school-district-math-pathway-and-advanced-calculus). CMU reads rigor against the school profile, so this is maximum available. Residual caveat: no AP exam corroborates the senior course.
    • Binding-ED-only early path (Nov 2) — given drop-candidate status, plan on RD.

    Placement recommendation¶

    • Category: Reach (overall ~11%, and CoE/SCS run materially harder).
    • Rank (his 1-4 scale): Program-prestige a clear 1 (arguably the top robotics program on his entire list). His own environment-fit view is a 3 — the intensity/stress fear dominates. Classic profile-vs-prestige tension.
    • Practical notes: Common App only; must apply directly to a college (CoE vs SCS — SCS is where Robotics sits and is the hardest door; CoE is the more realistic entry with robotics access via additional major). SCS requires SAT/ACT; CoE is test-flexible.
