    Cornell University College Research

    Cornell University is a private Ivy League research university in Ithaca, NY — a mid-sized university (~16,100 undergrad) that nonetheless functions through smaller colleges, with Runchen applying to the College of Engineering (~3,200 undergrad). It is his stated #1 and ED-leaning choice, sitting in the Hi Reach tier of his list. See the profile and preferences. On engineering prestige it edges past peers like Michigan and Case Western, but the cold and size cut against his stated fit criteria.

    Links: admissions.cornell.edu; Cornell Engineering (engineering.cornell.edu); Meinig School of Biomedical Engineering (bme.cornell.edu); finaid.cornell.edu/cost-attend.

    Field Value
    Total undergrad ~16,128 (Fall 2024)
    Engineering undergrad ~3,200 (college enrolls ~800 first-years/yr)

    Testing policy Test-REQUIRED — SAT or ACT required for fall 2026 and beyond (test-optional era ended); verify still required for his cycle
    Admission type Holistic; admitted to the College of Engineering, not to a specific major — students affiliate with a major at end of first year (verify)
    Application platform Common App + Cornell first-year writing supplement
    App plans & deadlines ED Nov 1 (binding; decision mid-Dec), RD ~Jan 2 (verify exact dates for his cycle)
    Essays required Common App personal essay + Cornell writing supplement (Engineering-specific prompts)

    Aid Need-based only; NO merit scholarships. Need-blind for domestic applicants; meets full demonstrated need

    Fit for Runchen — the strong side¶

    • Elite engineering with a top BME. The Meinig School of Biomedical Engineering is a strong, dedicated department directly matching his medical-engineering pivot (medical devices, drug delivery, bioinstrumentation) — aligns with his "needs to involve electricity" framing, comparable to Case Western and Michigan BME.
    • Stats are in range. SAT 1550 (EBRW 760 / Math 790) sits squarely inside the ~730–770 / 770–800 bands; 3.982 GPA and 10 APs are competitive. He clears the academic bar — selectivity, not stats, is the constraint.
    • Robotics-relevant ecosystem. Strong MAE/ECE/CS research and project teams (e.g., Cornell autonomous/robotics teams) give his 7-year VEX + Worlds spike a natural home and a compelling "why Cornell Engineering" narrative.
    • Delayed major affiliation is a feature. Being admitted to the college (not locked to a major) lets him explore ECE / MechE / BME before committing — good for someone still crystallizing the robotics-to-medical direction.
    • College-within-a-university structure softens the "anonymous large school" worry: the Engineering cohort and residential system create a smaller day-to-day community.

    Fit for Runchen — the concerns (weigh seriously)¶

    • Cold weather — a direct, tested problem. Ithaca winters are long, gray, and snowy; he got sick on his visit. This is his single most consistent stated dislike and Cornell is one of the coldest schools on his list.
    • Size exceeds his <10k preference. ~16,100 undergrad is above his stated comfort zone (though the Engineering college is ~3,200 and more contained than Michigan's ~35k).
    • Reach odds are real. ~8% overall and a historically tighter Engineering rate make this a genuine Hi Reach even with his profile; ED improves but does not guarantee.
    • Math rigor: no longer a gap, but needs framing. He reaches multivariable through Advanced Calculus (MAH802) in 12th grade; Bellevue HS does not offer AP Calculus BC (bellevue-school-district-math-pathway-and-advanced-calculus). Cornell judges rigor against the school's course list, so AB → Advanced Calculus is the maximum available track. The one soft spot is that Advanced Calculus is non-AP, so it carries no exam score — the Bellevue College dual credits are what substantiate it.
    • Test-required now. His 1550 is an asset here, but confirm the requirement (and superscore acceptance) for his cycle.
    • Cost / no merit. ~$99.7k sticker with need-based aid only — no merit money to chase; net cost depends entirely on the need analysis.
    • Culture is intense. Cornell engineering is rigorous and can feel pressured; collaborative pockets exist (project teams), but it is not a low-stress environment — worth a current-student gut-check against his cutthroat aversion.

    Placement recommendation¶

    • Category: Reach (Hi Reach) — ~8% overall, single-digit engineering, no residency advantage (private).
    • Rank (his 1–4 scale): Program-prestige view a 1 — top-tier engineering, strong BME, robotics-rich, and his declared #1. On his own environment-fit criteria (cold, size) it reads more like a 2, so there is a real profile-vs-prestige tension: this is the school his ambition ranks first but his stated preferences would penalize on weather and scale.
    • Practical notes: Applies via Common App with an Engineering-specific writing supplement; ED is his strongest lever given the odds (~54% of a recent class was ED). Admission is to the college, major affiliated later — his essay should sell "Cornell Engineering + robotics/BME," not a locked major. Confirm test-required status and deadlines for his Fall-2027 cycle before finalizing.
