    Boston University College Research

    Boston University — large private research university in Boston, MA. ~18,250 undergrads (a medium-large school, well above Runchen's "under-10k, campus-centered" preference) on an urban campus straddling Commonwealth Ave. It sits on his list as a Target — but Runchen himself marked it a drop candidate (Rank 4) for environment reasons (big, urban, cold). See runchen-wu-college-application-profile, runchen-college-list-draft-2026, and runchen-college-preferences-overall. Compare with the other Boston-area engineering options he's weighing, tufts-university-college-research and worcester-polytechnic-institute-college-research.

    Cal Poly SLO College Research

    California Polytechnic State University, San Luis Obispo is a large (~22,000 undergrad) public polytechnic and part of the California State University (CSU) system, known for its hands-on "Learn by Doing" philosophy and one of the strongest undergraduate-focused engineering colleges in the country. It sits as a Target on runchen-college-list-draft-2026 — a warm-weather, engineering-first, test-blind school where his stats profile is strong but which is much larger than his stated size preference. See runchen-wu-college-application-profile and runchen-college-preferences-overall. As a public in California, it is out-of-state for a Washington resident (unlike in-state university-of-michigan-college-research comparisons — note UW is his in-state anchor).

    Carnegie Mellon University — College Research

    Carnegie Mellon University — Pittsburgh, PA (urban). A reach school and arguably the strongest robotics-engineering fit in the US for Runchen's profile. Part of the runchen-college-counseling-working-school-list-student-profile; see also runchen-wu-college-application-profile, runchen-robotics-competition-journey, and runchen-college-preferences-overall.

    Case Western Reserve University — College Research

    Case Western Reserve University — Cleveland, Ohio. A reputation school seen as a good admission chance for Runchen. Part of the runchen-college-counseling-working-school-list-student-profile; see also runchen-wu-college-application-profile and runchen-college-preferences-overall.

    Cornell Bioengineering Major + Robotics Minor Pathway

    How to pair a Cornell bioengineering major with the Undergraduate Robotics Minor — arguably the single best on-paper vehicle for Runchen's story, welding his 7-year VEX robotics career to his robotics-to-medical-devices pivot.     Cornell Biological Engineering (BS) Undergraduate Program

    Cornell's Biological Engineering (BE, BS) major, administered by the Dept. of Biological & Environmental Engineering (BEE) and offered jointly through the Duffield College of Engineering and the College of Agriculture & Life Sciences (CALS). ABET-accredited. Relevant to cornell-university-college-research and Runchen's robotics-to-medical-devices pivot — see runchen-robotics-competition-journey and the parallel UCSD Bioengineering note. Cornell is a list school; essay angles in cornell sample essays reference.

    Cornell University College Research

    Cornell University is a private Ivy League research university in Ithaca, NY — a mid-sized university (~16,100 undergrad) that nonetheless functions through smaller colleges, with Runchen applying to the College of Engineering (~3,200 undergrad). It is his stated 1 and ED-leaning choice, sitting in the Hi Reach tier of his list. See the profile and preferences. On engineering prestige it edges past peers like Michigan and Case Western, but the cold and size cut against his stated fit criteria.

    Georgia Institute of Technology College Research

    Georgia Institute of Technology is a large public research university (STEM-dominant) in Atlanta, GA — warm climate, urban campus. It is one of the top engineering schools in the country, but with ~20,000+ undergrads it sits well above Runchen's stated <10k, campus-centered preference. He ranked it a 1 (top culture-fit) anyway — a notable profile-vs-prestige tension worth flagging against his "won't feel lost / worried about making friends at large anonymous schools" concern. On his list it is a Hi Reach. See runchen-wu-college-application-profile, runchen-college-list-draft-2026, and runchen-college-preferences-overall. Compare with peer research targets university-of-michigan-college-research (larger, also public/OOS) and case-western-reserve-university-college-research (small, strong BME).

    Harvey Mudd College — College Research

    MIT College Research

    The Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) is a small, private, STEM-focused research university in Cambridge, Massachusetts (~4,500 undergrads — well under Runchen's 10k ceiling). On runchen-college-list-draft-2026 it sits in the Hi Reach tier and he ranks it a 2 ("more of a grad-school place for me"); his parents want it. See runchen-wu-college-application-profile and runchen-college-preferences-overall for context. It's a genuine reach for anyone, and the profile-vs-prestige tension here is real.

    Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute College Research

    Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI) is a private technological research university in Troy, New York — small (~5,700–6,200 undergrads), campus-centered, and one of the oldest and most engineering-dominant institutions in the U.S. Runchen ranked it a 1 and it sits in the Likely tier on his list: an admit rate around the low-60s%, an engineering-and-robotics identity that matches his spike, and a size that fits his sub-10k preference. The main tension is location — Troy, NY is genuinely cold, which cuts against a stated dislike. See runchen-wu-college-application-profile, runchen-college-list-draft-2026, and runchen-college-preferences-overall. Compares naturally to his other tech-forward targets like case-western-reserve-university-college-research.

    Rochester Institute of Technology College Research

    Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT) is a private research university in Henrietta, NY (Rochester metro) — a large tech-focused school (~18,500 undergrads) built around career-integrated, co-op education. On Runchen's list it is a Likely / drop candidate (Rank 4): the engineering-and-hands-on-building identity fits him well on paper, but the size, cold, and co-op-heavy model cut against his stated preferences. See runchen-wu-college-application-profile, runchen-college-list-draft-2026, and runchen-college-preferences-overall. A warmer, smaller engineering peer he prefers is case-western-reserve-university-college-research.

    UC Berkeley College Research

    The University of California, Berkeley is a large public flagship (~33,000 undergrads, fall 2024) in Berkeley, CA (SF Bay Area) — the top-ranked public engineering school in the country, and a Hi Reach on Runchen's list. As a Washington resident he applies non-resident, which stacks a higher admission bar and zero financial aid on top of an already brutal rate. Because UC is test-blind, his 1550 SAT counts for nothing here — see runchen-uc-gpa-and-uc-campus-analysis for how he looks under UC's own recalculated GPA (capped-weighted ~4.29).
