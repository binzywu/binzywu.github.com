Question 2 — the "BME + robotics minor" plan, reality-checked

A real robotics minor for a BME major exists at only 5 schools

Robotics minor a BME major can actually declare

  • Cornell — MAE robotics minor, open to BME
  • Northeastern — ECE robotics minor, open to all students NEW
  • Georgia Tech — minor explicitly lists BME as eligible
  • Carnegie Mellon — Robotics Institute minor, open to CoE
  • WPI — Robotics Eng minor names BME as complementary

Everywhere else on the list, "robotics" is not a declarable minor — it's a concentration (BU, UCSD), a full major with no minor option (Michigan), an adjacent minor (Penn MEAM, Berkeley EECS), or just electives, labs, and clubs (Rice, UCLA, USC, Tufts, Santa Clara, UW, Harvey Mudd, Case Western, UIUC, the other UCs).

Of his current list, Cornell, WPI, Georgia Tech, and Northeastern deliver the literal "BME major + robotics minor" combo. That doesn't rule the others out — but the essays for them shouldn't be built around a minor that doesn't exist. Lead with the robotics-builder-for-medicine story instead.

Question 3 — essays (Fall 2027 cycle, with this cycle's changes)

Essay load is front-loaded and lumpy

Heaviest lifts

  • USC — 11 pieces (1×250 + 10 short answers)
  • Cornell — 6 required (2×200 + 4×100). Changed ↑ this cycle — dropped the pick-1-of-4, both short answers now mandatory. No university-wide essay.
  • Harvey Mudd — new prompts (500 + 250). Mudd screens hard for program fit; these matter unusually much.

Lightest / free wins

  • Case Western, UW — Common App base essay only
  • WPI — one 500 (likely no supplement)
  • Georgia Tech — Common App essay only; supplement eliminated this cycle — a genuinely low-effort app for a school with the BME + robotics minor

Strategy

  • Write one strong Common App essay on the robotics-to-medicine thread — it carries Case Western, UW, WPI, and the base of every Common App school.
  • Draft Cornell and Harvey Mudd early — highest count / highest fit-sensitivity.
  • USC's 10 shorts are quick but numerous — batch them in one sitting.
  • UCSD's 4 PIQs (350 each) are a separate voice — reuse robotics + biomedical material, don't restart.
Prompts finalize ~Aug 1; the wiki's master list needs a browser re-verify before drafting (a few rows predate Aug 1).

Question 4 — recommendation letters

Two teachers cover almost everything

The move: line up one STEM teacher + one humanities teacher. That single pair satisfies MIT, Harvey Mudd, and Cornell's subject rules — and covers every other school on the list at the same time.

Subject-specific rules to plan around

  • Cornell — 2 teacher + counselor; ≥1 teacher must be math/science/CS
  • Harvey Mudd — 1 STEM + 1 humanities; counselor optional
  • MIT (if added) — 1 math/sci + 1 humanities
How to request at BHS: Class of 2027 uses SchooLinks (not Naviance — retired) — request the teacher in the platform, not by emailing them through Common App. Each teacher writes one letter, once; the school sends it to every college — applying to 13 schools does not mean 13 requests. Counselor forms due Oct 1. Confirm the SchooLinks steps with advisor Lisa Hansen.

Counts by school

  • Case Western / Rice — 1 counselor + 2 teacher (Rice recommends one in-field)
  • Cornell — 2 teacher + 1 counselor
  • WPI / Northeastern — 1 counselor + 1 teacher
  • USC / Santa Clara — 1 (teacher or counselor)
  • Georgia Tech — optional / not considered
  • UCs (UCSD) & UW — do not use letters

The one STEM + one humanities pair covers all of these too. Ask both teachers before the fall term gets busy — a math/science teacher who has seen his robotics work is ideal for the STEM slot.
