## Exam and project

[Official exam overview](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-french-language-and-culture/exam): fully digital Bluebook exam starting May 2027. Free-response includes a project presentation (3 minutes preparation, 3 minutes speaking; 20%), four project Q&A responses (40 seconds each; 15%), and a source-based argumentative essay (55 minutes, two audio listening opportunities; 15%). Listening is 25 questions / 40 minutes / 25%; reading is 30 questions / 40 minutes / 25%.

[World-language revisions](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-world-languages-revisions): the 2026–27 project theme is music within Art and Creativity; the official packet is expected January 2027. The studio offers exploration and a blank evidence worksheet, not an official packet, PPR generator or prepared exam presentation. Learners must follow teacher directions and the current official project and AI policies. Our fictional passages are not authentic project research sources.

Timed rehearsal uses official task durations only. Our untimed production drills do not reproduce the format, full source sets, authentic audio, unknown prerecorded questions or scoring requirements of the official assessment. No AP score or language proficiency is inferred from completed checks.
