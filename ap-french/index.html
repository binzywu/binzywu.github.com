Six units, many perspectives

A path through the course.

The six unit themes follow the Fall 2026 AP French framework. Studio lesson numbers are our own organization, not official AP topic codes. These introductory exercises support, but do not exhaust, the course skills.

A useful study session

  1. Recall five expressions without looking.
  2. Read or listen for purpose before details.
  3. Complete both checks; ✓ Completed remains visible in the lesson list.
  4. Speak or write your response before opening the model.
  5. Follow the unit’s authentic-source link and note whose perspective it represents.

The redesigned 2027 exam

Project presentation: 3 minutes of preparation and 3 minutes speaking. Project Q&A: four prerecorded questions, 40 seconds per response. Argumentative essay: 55 minutes with print and audio sources. These three tasks form 50%; listening (25 questions, 40 minutes) and reading (30 questions, 40 minutes) form 50%.

Use the current official exam description. This studio is not a Bluebook simulator and gives no AP score.

Practice and privacy
