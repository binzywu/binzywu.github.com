Summer 2026 — Live Job Postings (Bellevue / Seattle)

For a rising high school senior (~17) · Compiled 2026-05-22 · Companion to the strategy doc
What this is. A live web scrape of Indeed and Snagajob on 2026-05-22 — actual currently-posted openings, not strategy. Pair with the strategy doc for the bigger picture (where to walk in, work permit rules, etc.).
Reality check on the data.
  • Listings are noisy. Many duplicate across cities; some are thinly-disguised MLMs (Vector Marketing, Spade Recruiting). I called these out in the "Skip" section.
  • Most postings don't disclose age requirements. WA lets 16–17yo work most jobs (with a school-signed permit), but assume you must call/email each employer to verify.
  • Pay shown is often "estimated" by Indeed/Snagajob, not the employer. Treat as ballpark.
  • ZipRecruiter and Glassdoor blocked the scraper (HTTP 403). Indeed and Snagajob were the only platforms that returned data.

WA state minimum wage 2026: $16.66/hr. Seattle minimum: $20.76/hr. At 16–17 you can work most jobs with a school-issued work permit form. No work past 12 AM on non-school nights; max 28 hrs/school week, 48 hrs/non-school week.
