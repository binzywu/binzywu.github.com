    Runchen Wu — College Application Profile

    Rising senior at Bellevue High School (class of 2027), Bellevue, WA. Contact: runchenwu@gmail.com · 425-240-2560.

    GPA: Cumulative 3.982 — no longer a perfect 4.0. A B in AP Literature & Composition, 11th grade Semester 2, broke the streak; every other course is an A. (Same subject where he scored a 4 on the AP exam.) Full record in runchen-hs-transcript-july-2026-final-g11; see also runchen-ap-exam-scores-2026.

    Immigrated to the US from China at age 3.5; Chinese-American and bilingual (earned a 5 on AP Chinese as a freshman).

    Self-summary: strong in math, science, and English; 7 years of VEX robotics as lead robot designer, builder, and programmer; advanced percussionist across SYSO, school band, and a private rock band; a team player with strong work ethic.

    Academics¶

    Strong STEM and humanities profile.

    SAT: Superscore 1550 (EBRW 760 / Math 790). Single-sitting score 1520 (EBRW 530 / Math 790).

    AP scores on record: Chinese Language & Culture (5, taken freshman year), Computer Science (5), US History (5), World History (5), Physics (5), Language & Composition (4), Calculus AB (5), Chemistry (4), Literature & Composition (4), US Government & Politics (4). Ten exams total — six 5s and four 4s, every score a 4 or above. See runchen-ap-exam-scores-2026 for the 2026 batch.

    Senior-year (2026–27) coursework: planned schedule is AP Macro/Microeconomics, AP Biology, AP Environmental Science, Advanced Calculus (MAH802), AP French, Senior English, and Band (Percussion Ensemble).

    Advanced Calculus (MAH802) is the post-AP-Calculus-BC course at Bellevue HS, dual-enrolled for Bellevue College MATH& 153 + 254 (multivariable). Completed math sequence: AL Geometry (8th) → Algebra 2 (9th) → Precalculus (10th) → AP Calculus AB (11th) → Advanced Calculus (12th). Bellevue HS does not offer AP Calculus BC, so this is the most advanced math track available to him — see bellevue-school-district-math-pathway-and-advanced-calculus.

    ⚠️ Open decision (2026-07-27): replace AP Environmental Science with AP Physics C – Mechanics if the schedule allows. Physics C – Mechanics (SCA701) is offered at Bellevue HS with prerequisite "Calculus or concurrent," which he clears easily. It's the most engineering-relevant AP available and it corroborates his robotics spike; Env Sci is the weakest course on the schedule and no school on his list needs it. If it can't fit, the cost is minor — his AP Physics 1 score of 5 already satisfies every physics requirement on the list, and no school requires Physics C. Full reasoning, caveats, and action items in senior-year-course-selection-decision-ap-physics-c-vs-ap-environmental-science-2026-27.

    Full record in runchen-hs-transcript-july-2026-final-g11.
