    Worcester Polytechnic Institute — College Research

    Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI) — Worcester, MA (~1 hour from Greater Boston). Known for its robotics program; a potential target school. Part of the runchen-college-counseling-working-school-list-student-profile; see also runchen-wu-college-application-profile, runchen-robotics-competition-journey, and runchen-college-preferences-overall.

    Links: Bachelor's in Robotics Engineering (wpi.edu/academics/study/robotics-engineering-bs); The WPI Plan (project-based learning).

    Likes (parents)¶

    • Robotics major; well known in the robotics community/industry.
    • The only ABET-accredited Robotics Engineering BS in the country with a dedicated department, dedicated labs, and a robotics-specific curriculum (not bolted onto ME/ECE).
    • Has Early Action (EA) — could be a good target.
    • Less burdensome Common App supplement.
    • 4+1 / BS/MS Plan.

    Concerns (parents)¶

    • Less national brand prestige.
    • The 7-week-term Plan is intense and unusual.
    • Smaller social scene.

    Runchen's Own Take¶

    Likes: strong focus on engineering and project-based learning; the famous "WPI Plan" emphasizes real-world, hands-on problem solving; dedicated Robotics Engineering degree (he sees it as relevant to AI + human-tech future fields); good preparation for industry jobs straight out of undergrad; Worcester is more industrial/quiet than Boston. Dislikes: less theoretical depth than some research-heavy schools. Worry: "Does it feel awkward?" — concern about the smaller social scene and fitting in socially (see runchen-college-preferences-overall).

    Current-Cycle Research (2026-07-24)¶

    WPI is home of the nation's first robotics BS program, a VEX sponsor, and famously project-based ("The WPI Plan"). Runchen ranks it #1 on his own culture-fit scale and it sits in the Likely tier — one of the cleanest program-plus-fit matches he has.

    At a glance (2025-26 cycle; verify for his Fall-2027 cycle)¶

    Field Value
    Total undergrad ~5,461 (Fall 2025)
    Overall admit rate ~60% (Fall 2024, 7,555/12,559); the "~69%" figure previously on file appears stale
    SAT middle 50% Not reported — WPI was test-blind 2021–2025
    Testing policy Was test-blind; reinstating test-optional for Fall 2026 entry (Class of 2030) onward — his 1550 should be submittable for his cycle, verify
    Admission type Holistic; NOT direct-to-major — declares Robotics Eng / BME after enrolling
    App plans & deadlines ED I 11/1, EA I 11/1 (dec. late Jan), ED II 1/5, EA II 1/5, RD 2/1. ED admits get a minimum $25,000/yr scholarship
    Cost (private) Tuition ~$61,790; total COA ~$82k/yr. ~96% of undergrads get grants/scholarships (avg aid ~$30,806)

    Fit for Runchen — the strong side¶

    • Best-in-class robotics fit. First US robotics undergrad program, only ABET-accredited standalone Robotics Engineering BS, and a VEX sponsor.
    • His medical-engineering pivot is served. BS in Biomedical Engineering with device/robotics crossover (e.g., the Medical FUSION Lab's robotic OCT kidney-transplant system).
    • Size and culture fit his stated preferences. ~5,500 undergrad, campus-centered, collaborative/nerdy STEM peer group.
    • Testing tailwind (new). With test-optional returning, his 1550 becomes a submittable asset again.

    Fit for Runchen — the concerns (weigh seriously)¶

    • Cold, gray New England winters — Worcester is colder/snowier than Boston.
    • Lower national brand prestige than his Reach schools.
    • Smaller, quieter social scene — his own recurring worry ("does it feel awkward?").

    Placement recommendation¶

    • Category: Likely (admit rate ~58–60%; his stats and robotics spike well above the median admit).
    • Rank: a rare case with no profile-vs-prestige tension — on his own environment-fit view it's a clear 1; on pure program prestige it's more of a 2 (elite for robotics specifically, lower general brand). One of his safest high-satisfaction bets.
    • Practical notes: EA I (11/1) for a low-risk early result; ED I adds a guaranteed $25k/yr scholarship if he's willing to commit.
