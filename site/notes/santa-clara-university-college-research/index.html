    Santa Clara University — College Research

    Santa Clara University — Silicon Valley, CA. Seen as a good target or even safety school for Runchen. Part of the runchen-college-counseling-working-school-list-student-profile; see also runchen-wu-college-application-profile and runchen-college-preferences-overall.

    Links: Undergraduate Degree Programs (scu.edu/engineering/undergraduate/degree-programs); Research Labs (scu.edu/engineering/labs--research/labs).

    Likes (parents)¶

    • Location: Silicon Valley.
    • Robotic Systems Lab (scu.edu/engineering/labs--research/labs/robotic-systems-lab).
    • Combined 5-year BS/MS.
    • Relatively good acceptance rate — a good target or safety; offers Early Action (EA).

    Concerns (parents)¶

    • Less engineering and overall school reputation.

    Runchen's Own Take¶

    Likes: close to Silicon Valley; more traditional college experience than hyper-competitive STEM schools (he notes this as an observation, not necessarily a positive). Dislikes: less cutting-edge feeling; fewer labs/research than big-name schools. Question: how competitive is the lifestyle, and how motivated are the students? (Motivated peers matter a lot to him — see runchen-college-preferences-overall.)

    Open Questions¶

    • Is it feasible to transfer to other UC schools like UC Berkeley or UCLA?
    • Confirm current early plans: does SCU still offer any non-binding early option, or only ED I / ED II + RD? (Parent note's "EA" is likely stale.)
    • Is engineering admission truly holistic-to-university, or is there any capacity/direct-admit nuance for impacted majors (esp. CS/ECE)?
    • Merit scholarship ranges a 1550/3.98 applicant could realistically expect (net cost matters given ~$89.7k sticker).

    Current-Cycle Research (2026-07-24)¶

    Santa Clara sits in the heart of Silicon Valley, ~6,500 undergrad — comfortably under Runchen's 10k ceiling. He personally marked it a drop candidate (Rank 4), but the counselor likes it as a warm SV-adjacent safety.

    At a glance (2025-26 cycle; verify for his Fall-2027 cycle)¶

    Field Value
    Total undergrad ~6,500 (6,484 Fall 2024)
    Overall admit rate ~44–48% recent cycles (42% for Class of 2027)
    SAT middle 50% roughly 1350–1490 composite
    Testing policy Test-optional (confirm still in effect for his cycle)
    Application platform Common App
    App plans & deadlines Regular Decision Jan 7; ED I (~Nov 1) and ED II (~Jan 7) — EA appears to have been discontinued, verify
    Cost of attendance ~$89,700/yr all-in; merit scholarships offered; avg need met ~70%

    Fit for Runchen — the strong side¶

    • Stats put him well above the bar. SAT 1550 is above SCU's ~75th percentile, and his 3.982 GPA far exceeds the ~3.72 enrolled average — a genuine safety/likely on stats.
    • Warm, campus-centered, and the right size. ~6,500 undergrad and a walkable campus directly satisfy his under-10k, dislikes-cold preferences.
    • Bioengineering major exists — a real hook for his medical-engineering pivot, plus a Healthcare Innovation and Design minor.

    Fit for Runchen — the concerns (weigh seriously)¶

    • He marked it a drop (Rank 4). His own read: "less cutting-edge," fewer labs/research than big names.
    • Bioengineering is solid but not a national BME powerhouse the way Case Western or Michigan are — no medical school on campus, smaller research scale.
    • Cost with likely thin aid. ~$89.7k/yr sticker; a high-stats full-pay family may see limited need aid.

    Placement recommendation¶

    • Category: Likely / low Target. On his stats this behaves like a likely school; the counselor's "warm SV safety" read is well-founded.
    • Rank: Program/prestige view lands around 3. His own environment-fit view is genuinely split: size, warmth, and collaborative culture argue for a 2, but he emotionally rated it a 4 (drop) on "not cutting-edge." Resolves toward keeping it as a comfortable floor unless he wants that slot for a better-fit target.
    • Practical note: No EA (verify), so the early lever is binding ED — don't burn ED on a likely school; apply RD or ED II.
