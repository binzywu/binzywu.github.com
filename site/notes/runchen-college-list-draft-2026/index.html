    Runchen College List DRAFT 2026

    Working draft college list for Runchen Wu (2026-27 application cycle), organized by category (Hi Reach → Reach → Target → Likely). Most targets are engineering / CS programs, consistent with his robotics and STEM focus. See also the broader runchen-college-preferences-overall and the counselor's runchen-college-counseling-working-school-list-student-profile.

Bold institution = a Rank-1 "won't feel lost" school (his top-culture-fit picks). A ~~struck-through~~ name = a drop candidate he'd cut first if trimming the list.

    Candidates recommended for addition (researched 2026-07-25)¶

    The list's structural gap: only one school under 3,000 undergrad (Harvey Mudd, 12% admit), while nine are over 20,000 — so his strongest stated preference ("won't feel lost," under ~10k) rides entirely on one reach. See additional-school-candidates-for-runchen-gap-analysis-2026.

    Institution Suggested tier Why
    Olin College of Engineering Reach (~19–22%) Best-fit addition found. 378 undergrad, all engineering, project-based from the first semester (matches his VEX builder profile better than anything on the list), ABET ECE degree + Bioengineering and Robotics concentrations, collaborative by design. Fixes the small-school gap at far better odds than Mudd. Caveats: tuition scholarship now $10,000/yr (was half tuition), mandatory Candidates' Weekend, no CS degree, cold. Dec 15 deadline.
    Rice University Hi Reach (8.0%) Better fit than several Rank-3 Hi Reaches already on the list: residential colleges + "collaboration over competition," top-10 Bioengineering with freshman-year design, Texas Medical Center adjacency, warm weather, SAT 1510–1560 (his 1550 is mid-band). Consider as a replacement for a Rank-3 mega-public.
    Rose-Hulman Likely (unverified) ~2,000 undergrad all-STEM with BME and EE — same gap-fill as Olin at lower selectivity. Needs research; site returned 404s. Potential swap for a Rank-4 drop candidate.
    Johns Hopkins Hi Reach (unresearched) #1 undergrad BME with deep clinical integration, ~6,000 undergrad. Best pure medical-device fit, but BME's intensity collides with his "too hard" sensitivity.

    Considered and not recommended¶

    Institution Verdict Reasoning
    University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign Likely drop (2026-07-25) Runchen's reason: myIllini is a separate application platform — the only school on the list not covered by Common App, the UC app, or Coalition. Reinforced by fit: Bioengineering ~#14 with no medical-device track (his key axis), no undergrad robotics degree, ~37k undergrad, cold. Michigan is the better keep in this slot (Common App, standalone Robotics major, stronger BME). Counter-argument: Grainger is #5 with EA Engineering at ~21%, and the myIllini burden is only 2–3 short answers with no personal essay.
    University of Wisconsin–Madison Do not add (researched 2026-07-25) Target (~41%), and BME's bioinstrumentation and medical devices specialization plus its every-semester design sequence are a genuinely strong fit. But 37,198 undergrad (largest he'd consider), the worst winter on the list, ~$62–66k nonresident with no residency break, and a big-sports culture he's said he doesn't want. Adds a fourth big-public-OOS entry to a category already covered by Michigan, UIUC, and Case Western. Add only if he wants a higher-probability BME-with-devices target and Case Western alone feels thin. See university-of-wisconsin-madison-college-research.

    What each school values: ranked admission factors (Common Data Set section C7) plus each engineering school's own requirements are collected in what-each-college-on-runchen-s-list-values-most-fall-2027-cycle. Headlines: character/personal qualities is a top-tier factor at six of his seven hardest schools; Rice rates all six academic factors "Very Important" (best use of his 1550); Georgia Tech rates state residency "Very Important" while targeting a Georgia-resident majority; UW rates residency "Very Important" but ignores recommendations and test scores entirely; and Cornell Engineering requires test scores for fall 2026 and beyond.

    Characteristics each school expects: the traits each school says it wants in an applicant (as opposed to how heavily it weighs each factor) are collected in applicant-characteristics-each-college-expects-fall-2027-cycle. Headlines: only four schools publish an explicit named trait list — MIT (eight criteria, and the best match on the list for a hands-on robotics builder), Cornell ("Honesty. Open-mindedness. Initiative. Collaboration. Empathy. Curiosity."), Olin (five C's), and CMU (institutional values). Collaboration is named by five of his hardest targets; empathy/service is named by five and is his thinnest area; Rice, Cal Poly, and the UCs publish essentially no character expectations at all. Michigan and WPI remain 403-blocked and unverified.

    Essay requirements: every school's writing requirements, deadlines, and word limits are collected in runchen-college-essay-requirements-master-list-fall-2027-cycle. Only Cornell, MIT, and Rice have published Fall-2027 prompts so far — the rest release around Aug 1, 2026 and must be re-verified then.

    Testing context: Runchen's SAT superscore is 1550 (see profile), which sits at or above the 75th percentile for most Target/Likely schools and within range for the Hi Reach cluster. Several UCs and Cal Poly SLO are test-free. Full AP record in runchen-ap-exam-scores-2026 and the profile.
