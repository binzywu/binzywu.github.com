## Introductory coverage map

Each unit has four grouped lessons. References are official topic numbers, while lesson titles, organization, examples, questions, model formulas and drills are independently authored. A listed reference means introductory treatment, not exhaustive coverage or equivalence to an entire textbook section. No released AP items or official rubrics are reproduced.

## Using the practice

There are 48 original checks and 6 original written drills. These do not simulate a full exam, reproduce its complete task distribution or establish an official score. Use teacher instruction and official released work for assessment depth. Model diagrams and numbers are explicitly simplified; no empirical forecast or observed result is implied. Unit percentages refer only to the official multiple-choice content weighting.

Completed-check flags are isolated to this subject. Written drafts remain session-only. All lessons and models run offline; external primary-source links require internet. No visual browser QA has been performed.
