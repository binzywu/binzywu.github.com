Reality check on timing. Most "prestige" summer programs (Microsoft Discovery, Fred Hutch SHIP, SYEP internships, hospital teen volunteer cohorts) had January–May application windows. By late May the door is closed for those — your best remaining bets are: city/parks rec jobs that hire rolling, retail/food, library volunteering, YMCA seasonal, private tutoring, and gig work. Items already closed are explicitly marked Closed 2026.

WA state minimum wage 2026: $16.66/hr. Seattle minimum: $20.76/hr. At 16–17 you can work most jobs except a list of "hazardous" occupations (no roofing, no commercial driving, restricted machinery). A signed parent/school work permit form (issued by your school) is required by WA L&I before your first day.

1. City / Government Youth Programs

Seattle Youth Employment Program (SYEP) Closed 2026

  • Age: 16–24, must live in Seattle city limits or attend Seattle public schools.
  • Pay: $21.30/hr min, 6–8 week paid internships.
  • Status: 2026 internship apps closed May 5, 2026; 250 chosen by lottery. Pathways track due Jan 15, 2026. Track for 2027.
  • Link: seattle.gov/syep · SYEP@seattle.gov

Bellevue Youth Link (City of Bellevue) Rolling

  • Not primarily a paid jobs program — focus is leadership, action teams (BYTE Back Tech, Youth Climate Action), Bellevue Youth Council, community service. Great for college apps as leadership/service.
  • Status: Council/board apps usually open spring; some action teams recruit year-round. Worth emailing now.
  • Contact: youthlink@bellevuewa.gov · 425-452-2846
  • Link: bellevuewa.gov · Youth Link

Bellevue Parks & Community Services — Lifeguards / Camp Staff Still hiring

  • Roles: Lifeguard (Bellevue Aquatic Center, Red Cross cert required), summer camp counselor, junior camp counselor.
  • Age: Lifeguards 15+ with cert; camp counselors typically 16+.
  • Pay: Lifeguards ~$19–22/hr; counselors $17–20/hr.
  • Apply: City of Bellevue careers

Seattle Parks & Recreation — Summer Jobs Still hiring

  • Roles: Lifeguard, Camp Counselor, Junior Counselor (Discover the Outdoors, Specialized Camps, Late Night, Get Moving).
  • Age: 16+ for most; lifeguards 15+ with current Red Cross cert.
  • Pay: $20.76/hr (Seattle min) up to ~$24/hr lifeguard.
  • Link: seattle.gov/parks/jobs/summer

King County Parks — Trails & Recreation Some open

  • Camp counselor, marine science aide, trails work. 16+, $18–22/hr.
  • Search "seasonal" or "youth" on kingcounty.gov careers.

6. Tech Internships for High Schoolers

Realistic-for-a-rising-senior framing: almost no Seattle-area tech company runs a paid summer internship for 17-year-old high schoolers. Most "high school programs" target rising college freshmen.

Microsoft Discovery Program Wrong audience

  • Targets rising college freshmen, not high schoolers (despite ambiguous web copy). Four-week paid internship in Redmond/Atlanta.
  • For a 2026 high school senior: not eligible this summer. Eligible summer 2027 after HS graduation.
  • Apps for summer 2027 typically open fall 2026 — calendar this for September.
  • careers.microsoft.com/students

Amazon Future Engineer (AFE) Apply in fall senior year

  • Scholarship + virtual learning, not an in-person Seattle HS summer internship. Apply for the AFE Scholarship in fall senior year ($40,000 + guaranteed paid Amazon internship after freshman year of college). Deadline late January.
  • amazonfutureengineer.com/scholarships

Boeing High School Internship Closed 2026

  • Boeing's Pre-MEP and Career Discovery Programs occasionally hire HS interns in Renton/Everett. Apps due Jan–Feb. Track for 2027.

T-Mobile, Expedia, F5, Concur, Smartsheet, Zillow

  • None publicly run a high school internship program. Stick to college pipelines.

What's actually attainable as a 17-year-old in May 2026

  1. Small startups via cold outreach. Search YC W26/S26 batch + Seattle. Email founders directly: "I'm a high school senior. I built [X]. I'd work ~15 hrs/week for free or low rate this summer." Hit rate ~5%, but one yes beats 100 LinkedIn applications.
  2. Open source contributions. Find an active project, fix bugs / add features. Visible on GitHub, free, signals stronger than most internships.
  3. Freelance on Upwork / Fiverr — under-18 needs a parent's account, but feasible. Web dev / Python / data cleanup pays $20–50/hr.
  4. Hackathons — Major League Hacking lists Seattle-area events; some pay prize money.
  5. Build & ship something. A web app, Chrome extension, small SaaS. Doesn't pay, but is the single highest-leverage thing for CS-track college apps.

8. Babysitting / Pet-sitting / Yardwork Gigs

Highest hourly rate available with the lowest barrier to entry. Available right now.

Babysitting $22–35/hr

  • Rate in Bellevue/Seattle 2026: $22–30/hr; $35+/hr for multiple kids or special needs. Date-night Saturday rates trend higher.
  • Care.com — must be 18+. Skip.
  • Bambino app — teen-friendly, parent invites only; ask people who know you.
  • Nextdoor — post in your neighborhood; include Red Cross Babysitting cert (free online via American Red Cross, ~5 hours).
  • School / community / synagogue / church listservs — highest-trust, well-paying.

Pet-sitting / Dog-walking $25–35/walk, $75–100/overnight

  • Rover.com — must be 18+. Skip directly.
  • Workaround: Advertise as a private dog walker on Nextdoor. Drop-in pet-sit visits $25/visit. Overnight pet-sits $75–100/night. Summer travel = high demand.
  • Pet first-aid certified ($30 online) — adds credibility.

Yardwork / Lawn / Power-Washing / Trash-out $25–40/hr

  • Mowing, weeding, bark-mulching, gutter cleaning, garage organization, moving help. Post on Nextdoor + door-flyer your block. June–August is peak.
  • Used pressure washer (~$150) → charge $200–400/house for half-day driveway/deck wash.

Tech setup / "geek squad" gigs $30–50/hr

  • Older neighbors pay well for iPhone setup, smart-TV config, photo backup, password resets, printer fixing. High signal-to-noise — 1 Nextdoor post often books 4–6 sessions.
